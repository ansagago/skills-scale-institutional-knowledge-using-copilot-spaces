# OctoAcme Project Management Docs

## Overview

OctoAcme's project management framework guides all cross-functional projects that deliver product features, services, and integrations. Our approach emphasizes customer value, iterative delivery, clear ownership, data-informed decisions, and psychological safety.

## Project Lifecycle

OctoAcme follows a structured five-phase lifecycle to deliver projects consistently and effectively:

1. **Initiation** — Validate business need, confirm measurable outcomes, align stakeholders, and decide go/no-go for planning
2. **Planning** — Break work into shippable increments, identify dependencies and risks, and establish timelines and milestones
3. **Execution** — Build, test, review, and iterate with daily standups, weekly syncs, and regular demos
4. **Release** — Deploy to production with comprehensive verification, rollback plans, and stakeholder communication
5. **Retrospective** — Capture learnings, convert them into actionable improvements, and measure impact

## Key Roles

Each OctoAcme project has clear ownership and defined responsibilities:

- **Project Manager (PM)** — Coordinates delivery, manages schedules, owns risks and communications, and facilitates team meetings
- **Product Manager (PdM)** — Defines outcomes, prioritizes the backlog, validates solutions, and measures success
- **Developers** — Implement features, write tests, participate in design and code reviews, and help identify technical risks
- **QA/Testing** — Validate quality and acceptance criteria, run smoke tests, and perform manual testing when needed
- **Stakeholders** — Provide inputs, approvals, and business context; participate in reviews and demos

## Core Principles

OctoAcme's project management is built on these five foundational principles:

- **Customer-first** — Prioritize customer value and usability in all decisions and trade-offs
- **Iterative delivery** — Deliver small, testable increments rather than big-bang releases
- **Clear ownership** — Each project has a named Project Manager and Product Lead accountable for delivery
- **Data-informed** — Measure impact and make decisions based on evidence, not intuition alone
- **Psychological safety** — Encourage open feedback, learning from failures, and continuous improvement

## Communication Cadence

To keep all stakeholders aligned, OctoAcme projects follow this communication rhythm:

- **Weekly** — PM + PdM alignment on progress, blockers, and priorities
- **Twice-weekly** — Delivery team standups (or as agreed by the team)
- **Monthly** — Stakeholder updates and business reviews
- **Ad-hoc** — Risk escalations and incident communications
